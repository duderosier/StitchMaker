# Device checklist

Run on the iPhone (Safari, installed to the home screen) and the Galaxy Z Fold 8 (Chrome; folded and unfolded) before calling a release done.

## Install and offline
- [ ] Add to Home Screen (Android: Install app); it is named StitchMaker and opens full-screen with the stitched heart icon.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, open the app from the home screen: it loads and works.

## Photo
- [ ] Choose photo offers Photo Library and Take Photo; a portrait camera shot is upright.
- [ ] A large (12 MP) photo loads in a couple of seconds.
- [ ] Photo step: one-finger drag moves the photo inside the outline; Zoom/Turn sliders and ⟲/⟳ 90° size and turn it; Fit to Blank resets.
- [ ] Every step: pinch zooms the preview and two fingers move it; the full-screen button and the magnifier (show whole preview) work.
- [ ] Scrolling the controls with a finger over a slider never changes it; dragging sideways does. The preview never changes size while scrolling.

## Background
- [ ] Magic wand: tapping the backdrop tints it red; the tolerance slider changes how much one tap takes.
- [ ] Remove border clears a plain backdrop in one tap.
- [ ] Erase and Restore brushes paint smoothly with a finger; Undo/Redo step back and forward.
- [ ] A subject lifted in iPhone Photos (touch and hold → Share → Save Image) loads with its backdrop already removed.

## Colors and editing
- [ ] The Colors slider updates the Pattern while dragging; specks cleanup levels visibly differ.
- [ ] Tapping a Floss highlights its Stitches; Merge, Swap (search "310" and "black") and Leave unstitched work.
- [ ] After editing (merge a color, paint a few Stitches), moving the Colors slider or the photo keeps those edits.
- [ ] Edit: single cells are easy to hit after pinch-zooming; pencil, eraser, Pick color, + Floss, two-finger pan/zoom, Undo/Redo in the top bar.

## Blank and Overcast
- [ ] Every Preset Shape and a custom size show the right outline and grid readout.
- [ ] Overcast: Edge tab appears; Ring Hole on a tab / inside / none; hole sizes and a custom size; tap to place the ring.
- [ ] Edge color: one color (pick from all DMC), Match design, and painting single edge stitches.
- [ ] Cut-Out: Margin 0–2 grows the cut line; the size line shows design and cut-piece sizes.

## Drawing without a photo
- [ ] Draw my own opens the Blank step (Photo and Background are hidden); pick a size and Fabric Count, then Edit has a few starter colors; pencil, eraser and + Floss work; zoom in to place single Stitches.
- [ ] Changing the Blank keeps the drawing centred; Overcast gives the Edge step (ring hole, edge color); Cut-Out edges follow the drawing.
- [ ] The drawing saves, reopens from My patterns, and exports a Chart.

## Importing a chart
- [ ] Import a chart takes a photo of a book page, a screenshot and a PDF (online the first time; then also offline).
- [ ] A PDF with several pages shows page thumbnails; picking one opens it.
- [ ] Dragging a corner dot shows the magnifier away from the finger; the purple lines can be lined up on the printed grid.
- [ ] Counts are suggested; − / + and typing change them and the overlay follows.
- [ ] Read chart lists the colors with close-ups; typing a DMC number shows its name; Pick and Bare fabric work.
- [ ] The Pattern matches the chart (Original toggles the straightened chart over it); Fabric Count changes keep every stitch.
- [ ] It reopens from My patterns; Re-read chart reopens the grid and warns before discarding edits.
- [ ] A preset Blank smaller than the chart shrinks it to fit inside the shape; Crop keeps it full size instead; switching back and forth loses nothing.

## Chart and saving
- [ ] Create Chart PDF opens the share sheet on iPhone (Save to Files, Print); downloads on Android.
- [ ] The PDF is one Letter page; Black and white has symbols only; legend counts look right.
- [ ] Swipe the app away and reopen: the pattern is back exactly as left.
- [ ] My patterns: open, rename and delete; New pattern starts fresh.

## Layout
- [ ] iPhone: one column; plum top pill; step chips under the preview; the drawer handle pulls the controls up; the bottom dock (Picture, Import chart, Draw, Save) is reachable with one thumb.
- [ ] Fold unfolded: Pattern on the left, step chips, controls and dock on the right; folding back switches to one column without losing work.
- [ ] Nunito loads (rounded letters) online and still shows after going offline.

## Split Cells and HEIC (2026-10-20)
- [ ] Import a chart with ¾ stitches: split squares show as two triangles on Edit, and the Split tool is already on.
- [ ] Edit → Extra stitch types On → Split: tap each half of a square, change the diagonal, tap a half again to clear it.
- [ ] Chart PDF shows split squares with a diagonal and two small symbols; the legend lists "+ N ½" for split halves.
- [ ] Choose photo and Import chart both accept a .heic photo (Chrome/DuckDuckGo need internet the first time).
- [ ] DuckDuckGo with the address bar at the bottom: the dock buttons sit above the bar.
- [ ] Import a chart with black outlines: the threads step lists "Backstitch lines" with 310; the pattern shows them as lines.
- [ ] Edit → Line: tap a corner, tap more corners to chain, tap the last one to stop; tap a line to remove it.
- [ ] Chart PDF prints the lines and a Backstitch section with lengths.
