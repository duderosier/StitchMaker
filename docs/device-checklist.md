# Device checklist

Run on the iPhone (Safari, installed to the home screen) and the Galaxy Z Fold 8 (Chrome; folded and unfolded) before calling a release done.

## Install and offline
- [ ] Add to Home Screen; the app opens full-screen with the stitched icon.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, open the app from the home screen: it loads and works.

## Photo
- [ ] Choose photo offers Photo Library and Take Photo; a portrait camera shot is upright.
- [ ] A large (12 MP) photo loads in a couple of seconds.
- [ ] Photo step: one-finger drag moves the photo inside the outline; Zoom/Turn sliders and ⟲/⟳ 90° size and turn it; Fit to Blank resets.
- [ ] Every step: pinch zooms the preview and two fingers move it; the full-screen button and the magnifier (show whole preview) work.
- [ ] Scrolling the controls with a finger over a slider never changes it; dragging sideways does. The preview never changes size while scrolling.

## Background
- [ ] Magic wand: tapping the backdrop tints it red; the tolerance slider changes how much one tap takes.
- [ ] Remove border clears a plain backdrop in one tap.
- [ ] Erase and Restore brushes paint smoothly with a finger; Undo/Redo step back and forward.
- [ ] A subject lifted in iPhone Photos (touch and hold → Share → Save Image) loads with its backdrop already removed.

## Colors and editing
- [ ] The Colors slider updates the Pattern while dragging; specks cleanup levels visibly differ.
- [ ] Tapping a Floss highlights its Stitches; Merge, Swap (search "310" and "black") and Leave unstitched work.
- [ ] After editing, moving the Colors slider asks before discarding edits; Cancel keeps them.
- [ ] Edit: single cells are easy to hit after pinch-zooming; pencil, eraser, Pick color, + Floss, two-finger pan/zoom, Undo/Redo in the top bar.

## Blank and Overcast
- [ ] Every Preset Shape and a custom size show the right outline and grid readout.
- [ ] Overcast: Edge tab appears; Ring Hole on a tab / inside / none; hole sizes and a custom size; tap to place the ring.
- [ ] Edge color: one color (pick from all DMC), Match design, and painting single edge stitches.
- [ ] Cut-Out: Margin 0–2 grows the cut line; the size line shows design and cut-piece sizes.

## Drawing without a photo
- [ ] Draw my own opens Edit with a few starter colors; pencil, eraser and + Floss work; zoom in to place single Stitches.
- [ ] Changing the Blank keeps the drawing where it fits; Overcast and Cut-Out edges follow the drawing.
- [ ] The drawing saves, reopens from My patterns, and exports a Chart.

## Chart and saving
- [ ] Create Chart PDF opens the share sheet on iPhone (Save to Files, Print); downloads on Android.
- [ ] The PDF is one Letter page; Black and white has symbols only; legend counts look right.
- [ ] Swipe the app away and reopen: the pattern is back exactly as left.
- [ ] My patterns: open, rename and delete; New pattern starts fresh.

## Layout
- [ ] iPhone: one column, all seven steps reachable in the bottom bar.
- [ ] Fold unfolded: Pattern on the left, controls on the right; folding back switches to one column without losing work.
